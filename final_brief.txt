Final launch-readiness audit brief

Perform a final implementation and launch-readiness audit of the completed platform.

This is a verification audit, not a planning exercise.
Assess the current codebase, schema, services, routes, UI, emails, scheduled jobs, admin tooling and user journeys against the final user requirements, compliance obligations and UX expectations.

Be strict. Do not assume a control exists unless it is evidenced in code or active operational wiring.

Required output for every audit item

For each item below, return:

Status: Pass / Partial / Fail
Risk: Critical / High / Medium / Low
Evidence: exact file paths, models, routes, services, pages, email templates, jobs, or config
Behaviour verified: what actually happens in product terms
Mismatch: requirement vs implementation gap
Fix required: only for Partial / Fail
Launch blocker: Yes / No
Required final sections

End with:

Critical launch blockers
High-risk compliance gaps
UX friction and trust issues
Operational weaknesses
Contradictions between code, UI, emails and policy-linked wording
Security and privacy weaknesses
Accessibility gaps
Performance/reliability concerns
Final launch recommendation: Not ready / Ready with caveats / Ready
1. Authority, access control and instructor scoping

Audit the authority model end to end.

Verify that:
Contentful instructor profiles are treated as public-content only
operational authority comes only from Prisma role plus explicit assignment
a Contentful instructor profile alone never grants instructor/admin capability
owner_admin has intended global authority
guest instructors only access sessions, runs and dates they are explicitly assigned to
guest instructors cannot enumerate, search, or infer users outside their assignment scope
guest instructors cannot access:
privacy tooling
refund/dispute tooling
legal/publication tooling
global evidence tooling
unrelated moderation surfaces
public/member/assigned-instructor/owner-admin DTOs are truly separated server-side
no staff-only or sensitive participant fields leak through shared endpoints, joins, caches, derived payloads, or UI prefetch paths
Required scenarios

Test:

member with linked instructor profile but no assignment
assigned guest instructor
reassigned/removed instructor
owner_admin
normal member
Cover/override scenarios

Verify:

timetable default instructor
session-level override for a cover week
permission transfer after reassignment
old instructor loses scoped authority
public instructor snapshot/history remains correct after reassignment

guest instructors do not receive raw health declarations by default

no health data leaks into broad endpoints, logs, analytics payloads, or emails
retention logic is implemented and operational
retention anchors are explicit and correct
deletion and export flows behave correctly for health data
incident/dispute/legal hold logic prevents unsafe deletion
deletion preserves referential integrity using anonymised shells where needed
Verify DSAR/export and deletion against rights expectations

Audit whether:

access/export is complete, readable and auditable
deletion/anonymisation removes what should be removed
preserved records are justified and non-identifying where possible

This should be checked against ICO expectations for access and erasure handling.

4. Recording, replay, deletion and playback access

Audit replay against the actual product rule:

Product rule to verify
Recording is enabled only for small-group programmes
Standard live classes are not replayable
Replay is available only in the authenticated small-group programme area
Replay entitlement lasts until:
programme end date + programme duration
example: 4-week programme → replay for 4 weeks after programme end
example: 6-week programme → replay for 6 weeks after programme end
Replay must not be accessible through:
public URLs
general dashboard class history
generic email links that bypass entitlement
raw/direct Daily playback URLs
Replay must be auto-deleted by Daily after the replay period ends
Playback access must stop immediately when entitlement ends, even if remote deletion is still retrying
Verify:
only small-group products can be configured recorded/replayable
recording notice is shown on relevant programme pages and before joining recorded sessions
recording acknowledgement is required before room-token issuance for recorded small-group sessions
replay policy is derived from programme duration, not a generic hard-coded window
replay appears only in the relevant small-group area
replay entitlement is tied to the enrolled user and programme
assigned instructor replay access is limited to sessions/runs they taught or are assigned to
owner_admin replay controls are properly scoped and audited
playback access ends on time
Daily deletion is triggered, retried on failure and audited
local replay state remains correct if Daily sync or deletion fails
no public or permanent replay URLs are exposed

Also verify that the copy shown to users, the config, the entitlement logic and the deletion job all derive from the same authoritative path.

6. Email audit: transactional vs marketing

Audit every outbound email and classify it.

Classification rule

Classify each email as either:

Transactional / service
Marketing

Be strict. If a message contains promotional content, product upsell, or direct marketing elements, it may count as direct marketing even if its main purpose is service-related. ICO guidance says if a service message has direct marketing elements, it counts as direct marketing.

Transactional / service emails to audit

Verify trigger, recipient, copy and logging for:

passwordless sign-in / auth code
account verification / onboarding
booking confirmation
calendar invite / ICS delivery
waitlist promotion
booking cancellation confirmation
class cancellation notification
instructor first-booking notification
empty-class auto-cancel notification
membership checkout confirmation
trial start confirmation
trial reminder / compliance notice / renewal reminder
failed payment notice
receipt / invoice
retreat booking confirmation
retreat balance due reminder if applicable
small-group enrolment confirmation
replay availability notice for enrolled small-group users
DSAR export ready
deletion request update/completion
dispute / refund operational notices
Marketing emails to audit

Verify consent, suppression and audience for:

newsletter sends
new blog post announcement emails
lead magnet follow-up sequences that promote services
class/programme/retreat launch announcements
promotional offers or discounts
win-back or “come back” emails
campaign sends triggered from Contentful newsletter content
Verify for every email
trigger source
audience source
classification correctness
whether marketing consent is required and respected
whether unsubscribe link is present when required
whether unsubscribed users are suppressed correctly
whether transactional emails still send when the user has opted out of marketing
whether delivery metadata is logged for evidence
whether any “transactional” email contains promotional copy that would reclassify it as marketing

Under ICO PECR guidance, unsolicited email marketing to individuals usually requires specific consent unless a narrow soft opt-in applies. Consent must be freely given and making email marketing consent a condition of purchase is unlikely to be valid.
